- **Followed posts on Artists, Characters and Copyrights.** Their pages have a **Followed posts** tab. **Check new posts** checks every site they're followed on at once and lists the posts, each marked with its site's icon. If one isn't followed yet, the tab explains how to add a follow.
- **Select and mark posts.** Tick posts to mark them seen or unseen together, or use **Mark all seen**.
- **Mark followed posts seen once shown** (Following → Follows & settings, or Booru settings; off by default): followed posts are marked seen as you look at them, including in the Booru browser, so next time only newer posts show as new.
- **Following** now opens on your follows and settings; **Posts from follows** lists posts from every follow.
- When the saved-post cache is full, the oldest posts you've already seen make room instead of stopping checks. Thumbnails are still loaded from the sites, not saved.
