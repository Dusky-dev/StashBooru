- **Following is experimental and off by default.** Turn it on in Settings › Experimental › Following (shown in Advanced mode). While it is off, the Following page, Follow buttons, Followed posts tabs and new-post counts are hidden and automatic checks stop. Your follows and check settings are kept, so turning it back on picks up where you left off.
- **Gallery or list for Booru post media.** A post with several media has a **List / Gallery** switch. Gallery shows every media as rows of thumbnails; choose one to see it in the list. The choice is remembered in this browser.
- **Shorter media lists.** A long list shows the first file, the open one with the files before and after it, and the last; **…** shows the rest.
