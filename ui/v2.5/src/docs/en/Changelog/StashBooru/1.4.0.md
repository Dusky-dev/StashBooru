- **Automatic follow checks.** In Following → Manage, choose **Check follows** every 1, 3, 6, 12 or 24 hours (off until you turn it on). StashBooru then checks your follows in the background, and **Check all now** runs one right away. It shows when the last check ran and when the next one is due.
- **New-post counts.** The Following link in the navbar shows how many new posts are waiting. Each follow shows its own count, and **Following** buttons say how many are new.
- If a site asks StashBooru to slow down, its other follows wait until the next check. One failing follow doesn't stop the others.
